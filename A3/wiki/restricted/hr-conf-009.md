The Disciplinary Register 2026 is a confidential HR-only register containing disciplinary case entries across Northbridge Cycles sites and departments [^r1]. Employee names are held strictly in separate HR case files rather than within the register itself [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, the register tracks eight recorded disciplinary cases from 2026 covering stores, assembly, purchasing, sales, and quality [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r9].

## Clauses

### Register Structure and Case Anonymity
The Disciplinary Register 2026 is maintained as a confidential, HR-only log where employee names are not listed directly and are instead retained in separate HR case files [^r1].

### Case DC-2026-001
Case DC-2026-001 was opened on 19 January 2026 in Manchester stores concerning lateness [^r2]. The matter concluded with an informal outcome and is closed [^r2].

### Case DC-2026-002
Case DC-2026-002 was opened on 3 February 2026 in Leeds assembly for a safety breach involving operating a forklift without a licence [^r3]. The case resulted in a first written warning and is closed [^r3].

### Case DC-2026-003
Case DC-2026-003 was opened on 27 March 2026 in Manchester assembly regarding absence [^r4]. It concluded with an informal outcome and is closed [^r4].

### Case DC-2026-004
Case DC-2026-004 was opened on 12 May 2026 in Purchasing regarding an undeclared gift under [[pol-abc-011|POL-ABC-011]] [^r5]. The case resulted in a first written warning and is closed [^r5].

### Case DC-2026-005
Case DC-2026-005 was opened on 30 June 2026 in Manchester stores after an incident where a dropped battery pack was not reported [^r6]. The action resulted in a final written warning that is currently under appeal [^r6].

### Case DC-2026-006
Case DC-2026-006 was opened on 21 August 2026 in Sales regarding the misuse of a company card [^r7]. It has an investigation outcome and remains open [^r7].

### Case DC-2026-007
Case DC-2026-007 was opened on 14 September 2026 in Quality regarding a falsified inspection record [^r8]. The proceeding resulted in dismissal and is closed [^r8].

### Case DC-2026-008
Case DC-2026-008 was opened on 6 October 2026 in Leeds stores for lateness [^r9]. It resulted in an informal outcome and remains open [^r9].

## Who it applies to

This register applies strictly to Human Resources personnel managing disciplinary actions across all Northbridge Cycles facilities and operating departments, with underlying subject identities stored exclusively in HR case files [^r1].
