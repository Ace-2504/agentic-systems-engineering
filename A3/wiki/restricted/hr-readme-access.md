This access notice governs the confidentiality and usage rules for the confidential HR folder as of 26 October 2026. The folder is strictly restricted to members of the NBC-HR-CONF group.[^r1] Any unauthorized access must be reported immediately to the IT service desk, and contents must never be duplicated or indexed.[^r2][^r3]

Code: README_ACCESS.txt
Target group: NBC-HR-CONF
Contact extension: 2210
Governing rule: POL-ITS-008 section 5
Status: current

## Clauses

### 1. Folder Access Restriction
The confidential HR folder is strictly restricted to the HR group NBC-HR-CONF.[^r1]

### 2. Unauthorized Access Reporting
Anyone who is not in HR and can read files in the confidential HR folder must report it to the IT service desk at extension 2210 and immediately close the folder.[^r2]

### 3. Prohibition on Duplication and Indexing
Files in the confidential HR folder must not be copied, forwarded, or indexed pursuant to [[pol-its-008|POL-ITS-008 IT Security Policy]] section 5.[^r3]

## Who it applies to
These rules apply to the HR group NBC-HR-CONF as authorized users, as well as any non-HR personnel who inadvertently gain access to the confidential HR folder.[^r1][^r2]
