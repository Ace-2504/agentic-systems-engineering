Document HR-CONF-007 v1.0 sets the confidential salary bands, variable pay guidelines, and annual increment rules for the 2026-27 cycle [^r1]. As of Monday 26 October 2026, this policy is current, effective, and strictly restricted to the HR department [^r1][^r2]. It governs annual compensation ranges for purchase roles and the plant head [^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6].

## Clauses

### §1. Use of this page
HR-CONF-007 is confidential to the HR department and must not be shared with employees, managers outside HR, or any system serving other staff [^r2].

### §2. Bands for the purchase department
The annual fixed pay for the Purchase executive role (PUR-EXEC) in band M2 is minimum £34,000 and maximum £42,000 [^r3]. Annual fixed pay for the Senior buyer role (PUR-SR) in band M3 is minimum £43,000 and maximum £55,000 [^r4]. The Purchase manager role (PUR-MGR) in band M4 has an annual fixed pay minimum of £58,000 and maximum of £75,000 [^r5]. The Plant head role (PLANT-HEAD) in band L1 has an annual fixed pay minimum of £110,000 and maximum of £145,000 [^r6]. Variable pay for the purchase department is up to 12% of fixed pay, paid in May based on the year's savings against rate contracts [^r7].

### §3. Increments
The 2026-27 increment pool is 9.5% of fixed pay, and increments above 14% for any individual person require the plant head's approval [^r8].

## Who it applies to
This document applies strictly to Human Resources staff handling compensation for the Purchase department roles (PUR-EXEC, PUR-SR, PUR-MGR) and the Plant head role (PLANT-HEAD) [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6].
