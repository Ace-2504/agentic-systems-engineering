HR-CONF-011 is a draft consultation plan regarding the proposed consolidation of the Leeds stores unit into Manchester main stores.[^r1] As of 26 October 2026, the proposal is strictly confidential and restricted to the steering group prior to any official announcement.[^r5] Under the plan, collective consultation is scheduled to open in January 2027 ahead of a proposed transfer of operations on 1 April 2027.[^r1][^r3]

## Clauses

### §1. Proposal
Under the draft consultation plan, it is proposed to move all stores work at the Leeds unit to Manchester main stores from 1 April 2027, placing six stores roles at Leeds at risk.[^r1] Physical inventory held at Leeds will be transferred to Manchester, and material requisitions for Leeds assembly work will subsequently be fulfilled directly from Manchester.[^r2]

### §2. Consultation timetable
Under the proposed timetable, collective consultation would open on 11 January 2027 and last at least 30 days.[^r3] No notice of redundancy may be issued while collective consultation remains ongoing.[^r4]

### §3. Communication
Nothing in the consolidation plan may be shared outside the steering group consisting of Henrik Weber, Bronwen Price, and Sofia Lindqvist before the announcement.[^r5] Any unexpected operational queries regarding Leeds stockholding are directed to Henrik Weber.[^r6]

## Who it applies to
This draft plan applies to the steering group—Henrik Weber, Bronwen Price, and Sofia Lindqvist—as well as the six stores roles at the Leeds unit proposed to be placed at risk under the consolidation.[^r1][^r5]
