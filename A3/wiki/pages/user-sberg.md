Stefan Berg is a production planner at Northbridge Cycles.[^r1][^r2] He is the designated author and owner of key production operating procedures governing work order management and engineering change processes.[^r1][^r2] As of 26 October 2026, his documented procedures remain current across production planning operations.[^r1][^r2]

## Role and limits

Stefan Berg serves in the operational role of production planner.[^r1][^r2] In this capacity, he authors and maintains Northbridge Cycles' standard operating procedures for shop-floor execution and engineering revisions.[^r1][^r2]
