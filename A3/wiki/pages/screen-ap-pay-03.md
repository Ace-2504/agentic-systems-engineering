Screen AP-PAY-03 is the Ledgerline interface used to review payment proposals generated by automated job JOB-AP-030 and to execute approval workflows[^r1]. Accounts payable staff inspect proposal totals, check individual supplier lines for disputes or debit notes, and confirm transmission formats before release[^r2]. It also surfaces rejected or set-aside lines through a dedicated exclusion summary pane[^r2][^r3].

## What it is for

Screen AP-PAY-03 is used by the AP clerk to review payment proposals generated by automated job JOB-AP-030 and by the AP manager to approve them[^r1].

## Fields

Staff must check proposal header details and reconcile payment totals with agreed cash allocations on screen AP-PAY-03[^r2]. In addition, staff inspect supplier lines for debit notes or disputes and verify the format STD18 electronic transfer assignment[^r2].

## Messages

The exclusion summary pane on screen AP-PAY-03 displays supplier lines set aside under error [[err-bnk-0101|ERR-BNK-0101]][^r2][^r3].
