SOP-IT-001 governs the process for requesting, setting up, and updating user access rights and operational permissions across the Ledgerline system [^r1][^r2]. As of 26 October 2026, all account creations and privilege amendments require formal initiation on form NBC-F-031 and strict single-role allocation [^r2][^r3]. Approval limits assigned to accounts must align directly with the purchasing approval matrix, and permissions remain subject to mandatory half-yearly reviews [^r4][^r6].

## Steps
1. The line manager initiates and signs form NBC-F-031 to request new permissions or amend existing access [^r2].
2. For requests involving access to financial postings, purchase order approval, or inventory adjustments, explicit confirmation and sign-off are secured from the relevant department head before credentials can be issued [^r7].
3. IT processes the request and assigns only a single role matching the applicant's job responsibilities, ensuring no user holds multiple operational roles simultaneously [^r3].
4. IT configures the user's financial approval limit based on the specific job title listed in [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] [^r4].
5. IT sets up default cost centres, which must later be updated via a new form NBC-F-031 submitted by the cost centre owner if Finance closes a cost centre [^r5].
6. Upon creating or modifying the operator account, the systems administrator records a cross-reference to the authorised request document against the user profile, files paper slips in the server room, and stores electronic copies in the secure administration folder [^r8].
7. Line managers conduct a formal review of all assigned user access permissions every six months [^r6].

## Forms and screens
* **NBC-F-031**: Used by line managers to initiate Ledgerline access requests or amendments, and by cost centre owners to request changes to default cost centres [^r2][^r5].

## Checks and approvals
* **Line manager signature**: Required on form NBC-F-031 before IT can initiate setup or process any account changes [^r2].
* **Department head sign-off**: Explicit confirmation and sign-off are required from the department head prior to granting access to financial postings, purchase order approvals, or inventory adjustments [^r7].
* **Role assignment check**: IT validates that only one operational role is granted per applicant matching their responsibilities [^r3].
* **Approval matrix validation**: The user's financial approval limit is verified against [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] according to job title [^r4].
* **Periodic review**: Line managers must execute a review of user permissions every six months [^r6].
