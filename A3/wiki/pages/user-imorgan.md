Isla Morgan is the sales manager at Northbridge Cycles as of 26 October 2026 [^r1]. She serves as the author and process owner for customer sales order processing and warranty claim management [^r1][^r2]. Her ownership covers order fulfilment through despatch as well as returns merchandise authorization procedures [^r1][^r2].

## Role and limits
Isla Morgan holds the role of sales manager [^r1]. She owns and maintains standard operating procedure documentation for sales despatch operations and warranty claims handling [^r1][^r2].
