POL-TEX-007 governs company travel and reimbursement of business expenses across Northbridge Cycles [^r1][^r2]. Version 2.4 is currently in force, setting hotel caps, mileage reimbursement rates, and expense claim procedures [^r1][^r4][^r5][^r9]. Version 3.0 has been issued but does not take effect until 1 January 2027 [^r10].

## Who it applies to

The policy applies to all permanent staff, temporary workers, and contractors operating from the Manchester factory, the Leeds unit, or travelling on business elsewhere [^r2].

## Clauses

### 1. Scope

Version 2.4 of POL-TEX-007 took effect on 1 July 2024, authored and approved by Financial Controller [[user-gwhitaker|Grace Whitaker]] to update hotel caps [^r1]. Expenditure must arise wholly and necessarily from company duties, and staff must secure approval from their department head before arranging journeys [^r3].

### 2. Mileage

Business mileage in a private car is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in the tax year and 25p per mile thereafter [^r4].

### 3. Hotels and meals

Hotel room bookings are capped up to £140 a night outside London and £190 a night in London, both including breakfast [^r5]. The meal subsistence allowance on an overnight trip is £35 a day, supported by itemised receipts [^r6].

From 1 January 2027, hotel room booking ceilings increase to £150 a night outside London and £210 a night in London, inclusive of breakfast and local taxes [^r12]. From 1 January 2027, if a hotel booking cannot be secured within the £150 or £210 ceilings, staff must obtain written approval from their department head before confirming the room [^r13]. From 1 January 2027, the meal allowance on an overnight trip increases to £40 a day with itemised receipts, covering dinner and incidental refreshments, and cannot be claimed as a round-sum payment [^r14]. From 1 January 2027, hotel room service surcharges, telephone charges, and pay-television fees remain personal expenses and cannot be claimed [^r15].

### 4. Flights and rail

Flights under six hours must be economy class, and rail travel must be standard class unless the journey exceeds three hours [^r7]. Inter-site rail journeys between Manchester and Leeds must always be booked in standard class [^r8].

### 5. Claims

Expense claims must be entered on screen [[screen-ap-exp-09|AP-EXP-09]] within 60 days of the expense, include receipts for every item above £10, and be approved by the claimant's line manager [^r9].

From 1 January 2027, version 3.0 of POL-TEX-007 replaces version 2.4, which remains applicable until that date [^r10]. From 1 January 2027, staff must obtain permission from their department head prior to arranging travel or incurring expenditure, and submit paper claims with original VAT receipts to finance post-travel [^r11]. From 1 January 2027, expense claims must be recorded directly on screen [[screen-ap-exp-09|AP-EXP-09]] within 45 days of incurring the expense [^r16]. From 1 January 2027, electronic expense claims entered in Ledgerline route to the departmental manager for review before moving to the purchase ledger clerk for payment processing [^r17]. From 1 January 2027, paper receipts must be attached to a printed Ledgerline system cover slip and deposited in the finance tray at Manchester or Leeds [^r18].
