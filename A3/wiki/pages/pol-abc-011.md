POL-ABC-011 defines Northbridge Cycles' standards against bribery, inducements, and conflicts of interest when dealing with suppliers, bike shops, and public bodies [^r1][^r2]. In force today, 26 October 2026, it sets declaration thresholds for gifts exceeding £50 and mandates plant head approval for hospitality over £150 per head [^r1][^r6][^r10]. It strictly bans cash, vouchers, and any gifts during active procurement tenders [^r4][^r5][^r7].

## Clauses

### 1. Principles
Northbridge Cycles prohibits offering or accepting inducements to influence commercial decisions, secure contracts, or gain improper advantage with suppliers, bike shops, or public bodies [^r2]. All gifts or hospitality offers must be declared through line management before acceptance, or immediately after receipt if advance notice was impossible [^r3]. Staff must decline any offer of gifts, entertainment, or hospitality made during active tender processes or price negotiations, regardless of value [^r4]. Employees must never accept cash, gift vouchers, or loans under any circumstances [^r5].

### 2. Gifts
Under clause 2.1, any gift worth more than £50 from a supplier or customer must be declared in the gifts register within five working days [^r6]. Gifts exceeding £50 are recorded in the central register held by the company secretary, and department heads decide whether items may be retained, shared across the team, or returned [^r8]. Under clause 2.2, purchasing staff must not accept any gift, however small, from a supplier participating in an open quotation or tender [^r7]. The gift ban during open tenders applies to all purchasing personnel, regardless of whether the individual is the lead buyer on that specific tender [^r9].

### 3. Hospitality
Under clause 3.1, hospitality worth more than £150 per head requires the prior written approval of the [[role-plant-head|plant head]], [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]] [^r1][^r10]. Written approval for hospitality over £150 per head must be requested via internal e-mail before making commitments, with confirmation retained alongside the expense claim or hospitality register [^r11]. The £150 per head hospitality threshold calculation must include travel, admission passes, and related extras [^r12].

## Who it applies to
The policy applies to all Northbridge Cycles employees, with strict prohibitions specifically binding purchasing staff—such as a [[role-pur-exec|PUR-EXEC]]—against accepting any gifts during open tenders or price negotiations [^r4][^r7][^r9].
