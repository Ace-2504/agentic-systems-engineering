AMD-SUP-2026-02 is an amendment to [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003 Supplier Policy]] owned by the Purchase department and authored by [[user-lmoreno|Luis Moreno]][^r1]. Effective as of 1 September 2026, it tightens acceptance criteria for UN 38.3 battery certificates to prevent failures in incoming abuse testing[^r1][^r2][^r4]. As of today, 26 October 2026, purchase staff must ensure that UN 38.3 certificates were issued within the last 12 months and maintain a validity margin of at least 60 days when ordering lithium-cell items[^r2][^r5].

## Clauses

### §1. What changes
With effect from 1 September 2026, [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]] §4.4 is replaced such that a UN 38.3 certificate is accepted only if issued within the last 12 months and its valid-to date is at least 60 days after the date the order is placed[^r2]. A certificate meeting the previous rule (issued within 24 months and not expired) but failing the new rule counts as no certificate for new orders placed from 1 September 2026, though orders released before that date remain unaffected[^r3].

### §2. Why
The policy change was prompted by a July 2026 incident where a battery pack lot from a supplier with a 22-month-old test report failed Northbridge's incoming abuse test after the cell maker had changed[^r4].

### §3. What purchase staff must do
Before raising an order for a lithium-cell part, purchasing staff must verify the certificate's issue date and valid-to date against the new 12-month issuance and 60-day margin rule[^r5]. Under [[sop-pur-005|SOP-PUR-005]], no new purchase order may be raised for [[items-bat|battery packs]] once fewer than 60 days remain before certificate expiry[^r8]. On the first working day of each month, the [[role-pur-sr|senior buyer]] must run the certificate-expiry report (MAN-PUR-72 §22.6) with a 90-day window and contact every supplier whose UN 38.3 certificate expires within that window[^r6]. All other parts of [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]] §4 remain fully in force[^r7].

## Who it applies to
This amendment applies to purchasing staff raising orders for lithium-cell parts and battery packs, and to senior buyers managing supplier certificate tracking[^r5][r6][r8].
