Warning W-BNK-120 is an automated hold placed on a supplier account in Ledgerline when a change to banking details is notified, as defined under [[sop-ap-004|SOP-AP-004]][^r1]. As of Monday 26 October 2026, it blocks all disbursements to that supplier until mandatory independent verification is completed[^r1]. Finance staff are strictly prohibited from bypassing or clearing this warning whenever discrepancies or doubts persist[^r2].

## Meaning
Warning W-BNK-120 holds all disbursements to a supplier account upon receipt of a bank detail change notification until independent call verification is formally recorded[^r1].

## Cause
The warning attaches automatically to the supplier account when bank detail changes are submitted or notified under [[sop-ap-004|SOP-AP-004]][^r1].

## How to fix
To clear W-BNK-120, staff must complete and record an independent call verification confirming the supplier's banking information[^r1]. Staff must never attempt to bypass or clear the warning if any doubts or discrepancies regarding the bank change request remain[^r2].
