Emma Clarke is a purchase executive at Northbridge Cycles operating under user ID ECLARKE [^r1]. She holds an individual purchasing approval limit of £250,000 gross as defined in the purchasing approval matrix [^r1]. Any purchase orders exceeding her limit, such as an order of £285,600 gross, automatically escalate to her manager [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] [^r1].

## Role and limits

Emma Clarke serves in the role of purchase executive ([[role-pur-exec|PUR-EXEC]]) [^r1]. Her direct purchasing approval authorization is capped at £250,000 gross [^r1]. Orders that surpass this threshold cannot be approved directly and route to [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] for managerial approval [^r1].
