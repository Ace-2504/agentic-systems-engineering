Callum Shaw serves as the Stores Manager at Northbridge Cycles.[^r1][^r2][^r4] He is the designated author and owner of core inventory standard operating procedures across warehouse operations.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4] As of 26 October 2026, his ownership covers standard procedures for goods receipt, vendor returns, periodic stock counts, and battery pack storage.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4]

## Role and limits

Callum Shaw holds the position of Stores Manager.[^r1][^r2][^r4] In this capacity, he authors and maintains operational warehouse procedures across goods receiving, vendor returns, physical inventory counting, and battery handling.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4]
