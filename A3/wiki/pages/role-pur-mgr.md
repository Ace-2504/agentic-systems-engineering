Role PUR-MGR is the Ledgerline role assigned to the Purchase Manager at Northbridge Cycles. Today, it confers a purchase approval authority of up to £1,000,000 gross including VAT per document. The role also serves as the mandatory sign-off authority for new supplier onboarding files, supplier unblocking, duplicate vendor handling, and rate contract administration.

## Role and limits

The PUR-MGR role carries a purchase approval limit of £1,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r1]. Commercial responsibilities held by the Purchasing Manager include overall authority for negotiating agreements, confirming annual price settlements, and authorising rate revisions in the system [^r7].

Only the purchase manager's office holding role PUR-MGR is authorised to enter rate contracts on the Rate contracts tab of the supplier master [^r6]. The purchase manager's office is also tasked with establishing the rate contract under [[sop-pur-004|SOP-PUR-004]] following supplier creation [^r5], which is verified in step 7 of checklist NBC-F-113 [^r8].

In supplier administration, role PUR-MGR is responsible for approving new vendor onboarding files and authorising written unblock requests submitted to IT [^r2]. Role PUR-MGR must sign box 7 of form NBC-F-112 before any new supplier record can be entered into Ledgerline [^r4], a requirement confirmed in step 2 of checklist NBC-F-113 [^r8]. In addition, accidental duplicate vendor registrations must be reported to the Purchase Manager (PUR-MGR) on the same day for blocking and document reassignment [^r3].

## Appears in

Role PUR-MGR appears in the following governance documents and procedures:
* [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]]: sets the £1,000,000 gross purchase approval limit [^r1].
* [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]]: specifies onboarding file sign-off, supplier unblocking authorisations, and same-day reporting of duplicate registrations [^r2][^r3].
* [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]]: mandates box 7 sign-off on form NBC-F-112 and handoff to establish rate contracts [^r4][^r5].
* [[sop-pur-004|SOP-PUR-004]]: assigns sole system authority to enter rate contracts and overall commercial responsibility for price settlements and rate revisions [^r6][^r7].
* Form NBC-F-113: details verification of box 7 sign-off on NBC-F-112 and rate contract establishment [^r8].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Approval limit:** Today, under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002 v3.0]], role PUR-MGR has an approval limit of £1,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r1]. In superseded version 2.0 of POL-APR-002 (2024), the limit was £750,000 gross including VAT (`superseded`) [^r9].
* **Supplier onboarding and unblocking authority:** Today, under [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003 v2.3]], role PUR-MGR approves onboarding files, authorises unblocks in writing to IT, and receives same-day duplicate notifications [^r2][^r3]. Superseded POL-SUP-003 v2.2 similarly designated the purchase manager as sole onboarding approver before entry, sole authoriser of IT unblocks, and recipient of duplicate alerts (`superseded`) [^r10].
