AMD-PUR-2026-03 is an amendment to [[pol-pur-001|POL-PUR-001]] owned by the Purchase department and authored by [[user-dokafor|D. Okafor]][^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, it holds the status ISSUED, NOT YET IN FORCE and will become effective on 1 December 2026[^r2]. Today, the direct purchase order limit remains at £50,000 until 30 November 2026[^r3].

## Clauses

### 1. What changes
Until 30 November 2026, the direct purchase order limit in POL-PUR-001 §3.3 remains at £50,000[^r3]. From 1 December 2026, POL-PUR-001 §3.3 is replaced so that a direct purchase order without a request is allowed only when its gross value, including VAT, is £25,000 or less[^r4].

### 2. Why
The amendment was prompted by an internal audit of the first half of 2026-27 which identified 212 direct orders between £25,000 and £50,000, many being repeat orders that should have been planned via requests[^r5].

### 3. What changes in Ledgerline
IT will update the Ledgerline direct-order limit from £50,000 to £25,000 on the evening of 30 November 2026 under MAN-PUR-72 §23.4[^r6]. From 1 December 2026, any direct order exceeding £25,000 gross will be rejected in Ledgerline with [[err-po-0610|ERR-PO-0610]], whilst direct orders saved before 1 December 2026 remain unaffected[^r7].

## Who it applies to
The amendment applies to staff creating direct purchase orders and the Purchase department at Northbridge Cycles[^r1][^r4].

## Versions and conflicts
- Today's rule: Direct purchase orders without a request are permitted up to £50,000 gross until 30 November 2026[^r3].
- Future rule: From 1 December 2026, direct purchase orders without a request are restricted to £25,000 or less gross including VAT, and orders exceeding £25,000 will be rejected with ERR-PO-0610[^r4][^r7].
