SOP-INV-003 governs regular cycle checks and the full annual inventory count across the Manchester plant and Leeds unit [^r1]. At Manchester, count duties are conducted by warehouse staff working in pairs, while at Leeds the unit supervisor organises counts using sheets provided by Manchester [^r6]. Cycle counts follow class intervals under plan INV-CCP-14, and full counts occur annually on the last Saturday of March [^r2][^r5].

## Steps
1. Cycle counts are scheduled by item class under plan INV-CCP-14, requiring Class A items to be counted every month, Class B items every quarter, and Class C items twice a year [^r2].
2. For the full annual inventory count, scheduling is set for the last Saturday of March, during which Ledgerline is closed to all stores transactions [^r5].
3. At Manchester, warehouse staff execute counts in pairs, whereas at the Leeds unit, the unit supervisor organises the counts using sheets supplied by Manchester [^r6].
4. Before counting begins, the designated count area must be frozen in Ledgerline on screen [[screen-inv-cnt-06|INV-CNT-06]] [^r3].
5. After counting, evaluate variances against threshold limits [^r4]. Stock counts whose variance by value is within 2% may be posted directly, whereas larger variances require a recount by an independent second person [^r4].
6. Compile the complete count pack consisting of original tally sheets, reconciliation reports, and written variance explanations for sign-off prior to final posting [^r7].

## Forms and screens
* Screen [[screen-inv-cnt-06|INV-CNT-06]] (Stock Count Entry): used to freeze the designated count area in Ledgerline before physical counting starts [^r3].
* Count sheets: tally sheets provided by Manchester to the Leeds unit supervisor to perform counts [^r6].
* Count pack: records consisting of the original tally sheets, reconciliation reports, and written variance explanations [^r7].

## Checks and approvals
* Stock counts with value variances within 2% can be posted directly [^r4].
* Variances exceeding 2% by value require a recount by an independent second person [^r4].
* The count pack must be signed by the stores lead and countersigned by finance before posting [^r7].
