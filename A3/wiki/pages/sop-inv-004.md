SOP-INV-004 governs the receipt, internal movement, racking, and staging of all power packs across the Manchester assembly works and the Leeds distribution unit [^r2]. Version 1.0 became effective on 2025-11-01, is owned by stores manager [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]], and was approved by quality manager [[user-pjansen|Pieter Jansen]] [^r1]. It sets the operational handling rules for [[items-bat|BAT-* items]] to ensure storage cell capacities, stacking limits, and FIFO rotation are maintained [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5].

## Steps
1. Battery packs may only be moved in their cartons on a pallet, and cartons must never be stacked more than four cartons high [^r4].
2. If a battery carton arrives without a legible date label, it must remain on the inbound staging pallet while an inventory clerk reprints the label based on consignment paperwork [^r9].
3. Battery packs must be put away only in fire cells BAT-Z1 to BAT-Z4, and staff must verify the cell's count board beforehand to ensure the limit of no more than 1,500 packs per cell is not exceeded per [[pol-bat-004|POL-BAT-004]] §2.2 [^r3].
4. If physical cartons in a fire cell exceed the board tally or adding a pallet would breach allowable limits, movement must halt, leaving the pallet in the transit lane while the stores supervisor is notified [^r8].
5. Any dropped carton must be quarantined immediately per [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002]], even if it appears undamaged [^r6].
6. Battery packs must be issued strictly first in, first out by the date on the carton label [^r5].
7. Workshop technicians must draw batteries only against issued build kits and return any surplus or unassigned packs to stores before the end of the shift [^r7].

## Forms and screens
Weekly stock counts and physical condition checks for battery stock must be signed off on paper tally sheets and reconciled against the system ledger before the end of the shift [^r10]. Cell count boards are maintained at fire cells BAT-Z1 to BAT-Z4 to track the cell capacity limit [^r3]. Inbound staging pallets and consignment paperwork are used to reprint missing date labels [^r9].

## Checks and approvals
Pieter Jansen approved version 1.0 of the procedure, which is owned by Callum Shaw [^r1]. Staff must check the cell count board before put-away to verify that the cell limit of 1,500 packs is not breached [^r3]. If carton counts exceed the board tally or a pallet would breach limits, the stores supervisor must be notified while the pallet remains in the transit lane [^r8]. Dropped cartons require immediate quarantine under SOP-QUA-002 regardless of visible condition [^r6]. In addition, paper tally sheets for weekly counts and physical checks must be signed off and reconciled against the system ledger before the shift ends [^r10].
