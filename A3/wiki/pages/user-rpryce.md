Rhys Pryce serves as the IT Manager at Northbridge Cycles as of 26 October 2026 [^r1][^r2]. In this role, he owns operational IT standard operating procedures and holds approval authority over IT security policy governance [^r1][^r2][^r3]. He is also responsible for operational oversight and quarterly sign-offs on system service account review routines [^r3].

## Role and limits

Rhys Pryce is the IT Manager responsible for approving core IT security governance and managing system access workflows [^r1][^r2]. He holds document ownership of user access management and service account maintenance procedures [^r2][^r3]. Additionally, his responsibilities include signing off on the quarterly review routine JOB-SYS-015 for system service accounts [^r3].
