Screen SYS-API-14 is the interface used within Ledgerline for issuing service account keys [^r1]. Keys generated through this screen are valid for at most 90 days and require mandatory rotation before they expire [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, it operates under the standard IT operational procedure for managing service accounts [^r1].

## What it is for

The screen is designated for the generation and issuance of service account keys [^r1]. Under [[sop-it-002|SOP-IT-002]], all service account keys issued via SYS-API-14 carry a maximum validity of 90 days and must be rotated prior to expiration [^r1].

The raw sources do not specify the individual input fields for screen SYS-API-14 beyond its function of issuing service account keys with an expiration threshold of up to 90 days [^r1].
