Luis Moreno is a senior buyer at Northbridge Cycles who authored and owns the current procurement procedures for supplier onboarding and certificate renewals [^r1][^r3]. He is responsible for verifying UN 38.3 test certificates and initial onboarding checklist form NBC-F-113 prior to order creation for lithium-cell parts [^r2]. Additionally, he executes scheduled monthly batch monitoring to track expiring certificates across a 90-day window [^r4].

## Role and limits

As a senior buyer, Luis Moreno is responsible for verifying uploaded UN 38.3 test certificates against scanned reports and initial onboarding checklist form NBC-F-113 before any order for lithium-cell parts can be created [^r2]. On the first working day of each month, the senior buyer runs scheduled batch job JOB-PUR-022 across a 90-day window to track expiring UN 38.3 certificates [^r4].

## Appears in

Luis Moreno authored [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]] v3.1, effective 2026-09-01 [^r1]. He is also the author and owner of [[sop-pur-005|SOP-PUR-005]] Certificate Renewal (UN 38.3) v1.1, effective 2026-09-01 [^r3].
