The PKG-* item family encompasses packaging materials and compliance labels used throughout Northbridge Cycles operations. As of 26 October 2026, items in this family are critical for fulfilling transport and safety compliance requirements when shipping hazardous goods. Specifically, standard labels from this group must be affixed to parcels containing battery packs before dispatch.

## Items

* **PKG-LBL-UN**: The lithium battery handling label used during parcel preparation [^r1].

## Rules that apply

Under [[pol-bat-004|POL-BAT-004]], item PKG-LBL-UN must be affixed flat to every outer parcel containing a battery pack, whether classified as UN3480 or UN3481 [^r1]. This rule applies to all outer packaging holding items in the [[items-bat|BAT-*]] family [^r1].
