POL-QUA-005 establishes the overarching quality management rules and standards across Northbridge Cycles' manufacturing sites [^r1]. As of Monday 26 October 2026, the policy enforces strict production check clearances before dispatch, specific incoming inspection sampling thresholds, and ISO 9001:2015 certification requirements valid through 31 May 2027 [^r2][^r3][^r4]. It also mandates procedures for non-conforming items and supplier performance reviews [^r6][^r7].

## Clauses

### §1. Our commitment
No bicycle may be booked out or manifested until every production check has been cleared in the system [^r2].

### §2. Certification
Northbridge Cycles is certified to ISO 9001:2015 for electric bicycle design and manufacture, with the current certificate valid to 31 May 2027 [^r3].

### §3. Incoming inspection
Lithium-cell parts are inspected at acceptable quality level (AQL) 0.65, whereas all other parts are inspected at AQL 1.5 [^r4]. Every sample battery pack must undergo an open-circuit voltage check using calibrated testing meters before acceptance [^r5]. Clause 3.1 establishes the Acceptable Quality Level (AQL) sampling plans used during incoming inspection on [[screen-qa-ins-01|QA-INS-01]] pursuant to [[sop-qua-001|SOP-QUA-001]] [^r8].

### §4. Supplier quality
A supplier whose rejection rate is above 2% for two consecutive quarters is placed on watch pursuant to Supplier Policy [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]] §8.2 [^r6].

### §5. Non-conformance
Defective items must be removed from the line immediately, marked with standard quarantine tape, and placed into the designated quarantine pen in Manchester or holding area in Leeds to update the stock ledger [^r7].

## Who it applies to
The policy applies to all operations, production, and quality personnel handling incoming goods, assembly, and dispatch at Northbridge Cycles sites, with governance overseen by the Quality department and plant head [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]] [^r1][^r2][^r7].
