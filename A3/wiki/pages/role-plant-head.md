The PLANT-HEAD (Plant head) role governs high-level operational and purchasing authorisations across Northbridge Cycles as of 26 October 2026. This role holds an approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document for purchasing approvals [^r2]. In addition, the post-holder holds specific oversight for compliance approvals covering commercial hospitality exceeding £150 per head [^r1].

## Role and limits

The PLANT-HEAD role has a purchase approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r2]. The role must review and grant advance written approval for any commercial hospitality exceeding £150 per head [^r1].

## Appears in

The role appears in the following policy documents:
* [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002 Approval Matrix for Purchasing]] defines the purchasing limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT [^r2].
* [[pol-abc-011|POL-ABC-011 Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy]] defines mandatory advance written approval for hospitality above £150 per head [^r1].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Purchase approval limit:** Today's rule (effective 2026-04-01 under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] v3.0) assigns role PLANT-HEAD a purchase approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r2]. The superseded v2.0 of POL-APR-002 also assigned an approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT to role PLANT-HEAD [^r3]. Sources: POL-APR-002 v3.0 (2026-04-01) [^r2] and 01_policies/old/Approval_Matrix_v2.0_2024.docx [^r3] (kind: `superseded`).
