POL-DRP-009 defines mandatory record retention periods, storage mandates, and secure disposal routines across all Northbridge departments [^r1]. As of Monday 26 October 2026, version 1.4 is current and strictly governs both physical paperwork and electronic Ledgerline transaction purges [^r1][^r4]. It sets explicit statutory retention schedules ranging from 31 days for facility recordings to 15 years for battery testing [^r9][^r11].

## Clauses

### 1. Why we keep records
Data Retention Policy POL-DRP-009 version 1.4 took effect on 2025-05-01, is owned jointly by Finance and IT, and was approved by financial controller [[user-gwhitaker|Grace Whitaker]] [^r1]. Paper documents such as goods received sheets and carrier delivery notes must be filed in departmental binders promptly at the end of each shift, and historical files must never be destroyed without written confirmation from the department manager [^r2].

### 3. Disposal
Confidential paper waste, old job cards, and superseded despatch notes at the Manchester factory and Leeds unit must be deposited into locked confidential destruction consoles on site for routine shredding and collection by an approved waste handler [^r3]. Ledgerline ERP does not automatically delete expired transactions, requiring system administrators to run manual purge utilities during scheduled maintenance windows after full backups when ledger years or master records fall outside statutory requirements [^r4].

### Table 1. Retention periods
Purchase orders, receipts, and supplier invoices are owned by Finance and must be kept for 7 years after the end of the financial year [^r5]. Supplier certificates including UN 38.3 and ISO certifications are owned by Purchase and must be kept for 10 years after the supplier is blocked [^r6]. The Ledgerline audit trail is owned by IT and must be retained for 7 years (84 months) [^r7]. E-mail records are owned by IT and must be retained for 3 years [^r8]. CCTV recordings are owned by Facilities and must be retained for 31 days [^r9]. HR personnel files must be kept for 6 years after the employee leaves, while job applicant records must be kept for 6 months after the recruitment decision [^r10]. [[items-bat|Battery]] end-of-line test results are owned by Quality and must be retained for 15 years [^r11].

## Who it applies to
This policy applies to Finance, IT, Purchase, Quality, Facilities, and site personnel handling physical and electronic records across the Manchester factory and Leeds unit [^r1][^r3][^r5][^r6][^r9][^r11]. It also governs system administrators executing Ledgerline manual purge utilities and managers overseeing departmental paper binders [^r2][^r4].
