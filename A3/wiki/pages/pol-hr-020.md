POL-HR-020 defines standard terms for daily working hours, annual leave entitlements, sickness reporting, and special leave across Northbridge Cycles [^r1][^r2][^r4][^r7]. As of 26 October 2026, version 6.1 is in full effect across all operational units [^r1][^r2]. Compliance with its notification timelines and attendance procedures is required for all staff [^r2][^r3][^r7].

## Who it applies to

This policy applies to all permanent and temporary staff at the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit [^r2].

## Clauses

### 1. Working hours

Under clause 1.1, the standard working week across Northbridge Cycles is 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday [^r1]. Staff record attendance using electronic clocking terminals or standard departmental timesheets [^r3].

### 2. Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave plus bank holidays, and up to 5 unused days may be carried over to 31 March [^r4]. Any untaken annual leave beyond 5 days is forfeited without payment, and carried-over days expire if untaken by 31 March [^r5]. For assembly and warehouse sections, cover must be agreed on the shop floor before an annual leave request is entered into the system, and staff must not make non-refundable travel arrangements until formally approved by their supervisor [^r6].

### 3. Sickness

If an employee is ill, they must telephone their line manager directly by 09:30 on the first day of absence [^r7]. A fit note from a medical practice is required after 7 calendar days of illness and must be sent to the personnel department without delay [^r8]. Upon resuming work following sickness, employees must attend a return-to-work interview with their supervisor and sign an internal self-certification record [^r9].

### 4. Other leave

For unexpected emergencies such as sudden bereavement or urgent family dependency issues, employees must telephone their line manager directly before the start of the working day [^r10]. Requests for scheduled circumstances such as public service or statutory duties require supporting documentation submitted to the department supervisor as soon as known [^r11].
