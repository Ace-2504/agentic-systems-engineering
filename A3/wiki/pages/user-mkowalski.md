Marta Kowalski is the Accounts Payable Manager at Northbridge Cycles. She exercises exclusive override authority on invoice matching holds and manages the final approvals for supplier payment runs. Kowalski also executes monthly purchase ledger period closes and owns the core Accounts Payable standard operating procedures.

## Role and limits

Marta Kowalski serves as the Accounts Payable Manager and holds exclusive authority to override invoice match holds [^r1]. When executing a match override, she must supply a clear explanation in the override comment field prior to final posting [^r2]. She is responsible for approving supplier payment run proposals on screen [[screen-ap-pay-03|AP-PAY-03]] [^r4]. These reviews and sign-offs must be conducted independently using her own credentials, separate from the accounts payable clerks who propose the payment runs [^r5]. Kowalski is also responsible for executing the purchase ledger period close on screen [[screen-ap-per-11|AP-PER-11]] on the afternoon of working day 3 [^r3].
