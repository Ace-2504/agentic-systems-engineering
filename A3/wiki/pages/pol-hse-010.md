POL-HSE-010 sets out the mandatory health and safety framework across Northbridge Cycles Ltd's manufacturing plant in Manchester and secondary unit in Leeds [^r1][^r2]. Version 5.0 remains fully in force today, 26 October 2026, defining core operational rules for incident logging, forklift handling, battery fire safety, and site access [^r1][^r4][^r5][^r7][^r9]. It establishes clear accountability from the managing director down to floor supervisors and visiting personnel [^r3][^r9][^r10].

## Clauses

### 1. Statement
The Health and Safety Policy establishes the safety framework for Northbridge Cycles Ltd across the manufacturing plant in Manchester and the secondary unit in Leeds [^r2].

### 2. Responsibilities
The managing director retains overall accountability for company safety standards, while operational managers and unit supervisors hold direct responsibility for local floors [^r3]. When hazards or near misses occur, staff must notify their supervisor immediately and details must be transcribed into the central safety log on Ledgerline ERP without delay [^r4].

### 3. Forklifts
Under clause 3.1, only staff with a current forklift licence renewed every 3 years may drive a forklift [^r5]. Only approved forklift licence holders may collect ignition keys from the stores office, and operators must complete a physical walkaround check in the vehicle logbook before starting a shift [^r6].

### 4. Battery fires
Under clause 4.1, if a battery pack smokes or burns, staff must raise the alarm, leave the area, and call the fire wardens, and only trained fire wardens may approach a burning pack [^r7].

### 5. First aid
Trained first aid personnel are appointed across both Manchester and Leeds, with clearly marked green stations containing treated dressings, eye washes, and bandages positioned throughout the sites [^r8].

### 6. Visitors and contractors
All guests, agency staff, and trade contractors must report to main reception to sign the register, collect a visitor pass, undergo a site induction, and wear high-visibility waistcoats and safety footwear outside office corridors [^r9]. Hosts must continuously supervise visitors unless an independent contractor holds an approved permit to work [^r10].

## Who it applies to
This policy applies to all personnel across Northbridge Cycles Ltd, including operational managers, unit supervisors, staff, agency workers, trade contractors, and site visitors across the Manchester and Leeds locations [^r2][^r3][^r9]. Specific operational requirements apply to forklift operators, trained first aid personnel, and fire wardens handling [[items-bat|battery]] fire incidents [^r5][^r7][^r8].
