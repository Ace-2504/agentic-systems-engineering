Screen AP-DBN-04 is the Ledgerline interface used by the accounts office to record debit notes against supplier accounts [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, it is required whenever a supplier invoice has already been matched and posted and funds must be reclaimed [^r1]. It operates under the provisions of [[pol-ret-006|POL-RET-006 Returns Policy]] [^r1].

## What it is for

The accounts office uses screen AP-DBN-04 to raise a debit note against a supplier account when the supplier's invoice has already been matched and posted [^r1]. This process reclaims funds owed to Northbridge Cycles for returns to suppliers [^r1].
