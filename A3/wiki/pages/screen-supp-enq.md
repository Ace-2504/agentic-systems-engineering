Screen SUPP-ENQ is a Ledgerline 6 screen previously used to search the supplier list. As of 26 October 2026, reference to this screen comes solely from superseded procedures. It is no longer referenced in the current onboarding workflow.

- Screen code: SUPP-ENQ
- System: Ledgerline 6
- Menu path: Masters > Vendors
- Purpose: Search supplier list
- Status: superseded

## What it is for
Under superseded [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]] v2.0, screen SUPP-ENQ was accessed under the Ledgerline 6 menu path Masters > Vendors to search the supplier list [^r1].
