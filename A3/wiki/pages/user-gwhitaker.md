Grace Whitaker serves as the Financial Controller at Northbridge Cycles [^r1][^r2]. She is responsible for reviewing and signing off accounts payable period closes, and holds formal approval authority over Ledgerline stock adjustment postings following variance investigations [^r4][^r9]. In addition, she authors and approves core financial policies and standard operating procedures across cost centres, payments, expenses, and data retention [^r1][^r2][^r5][^r7].

## Role and limits

Grace Whitaker serves as Financial Controller [^r1][^r2]. Under [[sop-ap-002|SOP-AP-002]], she is responsible for reviewing the preliminary aged creditors trial balance, reviewing proposed accruals, and signing off the final close in the system [^r4]. Under [[sop-inv-003|SOP-INV-003]], Finance must seek formal approval from the financial controller before any posting is made in Ledgerline to adjust stock records following variance investigation [^r9].
