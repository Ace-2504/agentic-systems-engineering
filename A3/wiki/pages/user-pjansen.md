Pieter Jansen is the Quality Manager at Northbridge Cycles as of 26 October 2026 [^r1][^r2]. In this role, he authors and owns standard operating procedures for incoming inspection and damaged battery quarantine [^r2][^r3]. He also serves as the quality approval authority for battery handling and warranty claims procedures [^r1][^r4].

## Role and limits

Pieter Jansen serves as Northbridge Cycles' Quality Manager [^r1][^r2]. In his quality capacity, he authors and owns core quality inspection documents, including [[sop-qua-001|SOP-QUA-001 Incoming Inspection]] and [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002 Damaged Battery Pack Quarantine]] [^r2][^r3]. He also holds approval authority over related warehouse and customer-facing quality procedures, having approved [[sop-inv-004|SOP-INV-004 Battery Pack Storage and Handling]] as well as [[sop-sal-002|SOP-SAL-002 Warranty Claims from Bike Shops]] [^r1][^r4].
