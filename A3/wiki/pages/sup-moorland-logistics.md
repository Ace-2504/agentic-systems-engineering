Moorland Logistics serves as Northbridge Cycles' contract carrier for dangerous-goods consignments [^r1]. The carrier operates under service code DGX for all shipments containing battery packs [^r1]. As established in [[sop-sal-001|SOP-SAL-001]], their specialized service ensures compliant handling of [[items-bat|battery]] consignments [^r1].

## Record
Moorland Logistics is designated as Northbridge Cycles' contract carrier [^r1].

## Certificates
The carrier is authorised to operate dangerous-goods transport under service code DGX for consignments containing battery packs [^r1].

## Rate contracts
Moorland Logistics provides dangerous-goods transit under contract service code DGX [^r1].

## Notes
The carrier is used for orders requiring dangerous-goods transport under [[sop-sal-001|SOP-SAL-001 v2.2]] [^r1].
