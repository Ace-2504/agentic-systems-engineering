Voltway Paints & Coatings is an independent supplier located in Rotterdam [^r1]. Under [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]], it is distinguished by city and VAT number from [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] in Eindhoven [^r1][^r2]. As of 26 October 2026, it is treated as a separate vendor record [^r1].

## Record
Voltway Paints & Coatings in Rotterdam is registered as an independent supplier distinguished by its location and VAT number from Voltway Cells in Eindhoven [^r1][^r2].

No certificate details are provided in the current source documents.

## Rate contracts
No rate contracts are recorded in the current source documents.

## Notes
The supplier has a distinct VAT registration and city to prevent confusion with Voltway Cells [^r1][^r2].
