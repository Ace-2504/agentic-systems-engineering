Ledgerline error ERR-APR-0009 indicates that no approver could be found within the system for an order [^r1][^r2]. It displays when an order exceeds £5,000,000 gross, reaching beyond the configured in-system approval limits defined in [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] [^r1][^r2]. The error signals that written approval from the Managing Director outside Ledgerline is required [^r1][^r2].

## Meaning
ERR-APR-0009 means "no approver found" within Ledgerline routing [^r1][^r2].

## Cause
The error triggers when a purchase order exceeds £5,000,000 gross [^r1][^r2].

## How to fix
Obtain written approval from the Managing Director outside Ledgerline [^r1][^r2].
