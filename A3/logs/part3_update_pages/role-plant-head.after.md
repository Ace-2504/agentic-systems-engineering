The PLANT-HEAD (Plant head) role governs high-level purchasing and operational authorisations across Northbridge Cycles as of 26 October 2026 [^r4]. Under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]], the role holds an approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r4]. In addition, the role provides compliance review and advance written approval for commercial hospitality exceeding £150 per head [^r1].

## Role and limits

The PLANT-HEAD role holds an approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] [^r4]. 

The role must review and grant advance written approval for any commercial hospitality exceeding £150 per head [^r1].

## Appears in

The role appears in the following policies:
* [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002 Approval Matrix for Purchasing]], which defines the approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r4].
* [[pol-abc-011|POL-ABC-011 Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy]], which mandates advance written approval by the Plant Head role for commercial hospitality exceeding £150 per head [^r1].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Purchase approval limit:** Today's rule (effective 2026-10-26 under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] v3.1) assigns role PLANT-HEAD an approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r4]. Under superseded v3.0 (effective 2026-04-01), the PLANT-HEAD role had a purchase approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT per document [^r2]. In the superseded v2.0, role PLANT-HEAD also held an approval limit of £5,000,000 gross including VAT [^r3]. Sources: POL-APR-002 v3.1 (2026-10-26) [^r4], POL-APR-002 v3.0 (2026-04-01) [^r2], and Approval_Matrix_v2.0_2024.docx [^r3] (kind: `superseded`).
