Role PUR-SR is the Senior Buyer role within Ledgerline, carrying specific authority for purchasing approvals and battery supplier governance [^r2][^r3][^r9]. As of 26 October 2026, a user assigned this role holds an approval limit of £500,000 gross including VAT per document under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] [^r2][^r9]. The senior buyer is responsible for verifying battery safety certificates, monitoring monthly expiry schedules, and conducting regular supplier performance reviews [^r1][^r3][^r4].

## Role and limits

The PUR-SR (Senior buyer) role has a purchase approval limit of £500,000 gross including VAT per document under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] [^r2][^r9].

The role is responsible for checking uploaded UN 38.3 test certificates before the first purchase order is placed [^r3]. In this verification process, the senior buyer checks uploaded UN 38.3 battery certificates against test reports and signs off checklist form NBC-F-113 [^r5]. Specifically, the senior buyer must verify that the UN 38.3 report was issued within 12 months and is valid to at least 60 days after the first order on checklist NBC-F-113 [^r6].

On the first working day of each month, the senior buyer must run the certificate-expiry report (MAN-PUR-72 §22.6) using a 90-day window and notify every supplier whose UN 38.3 certificate expires within it [^r1]. In addition, the senior buyer conducts quarterly performance reviews of regular suppliers covering delivery on-time, rejection rate, and price [^r4].

## Versions and conflicts

* Today's rule: Role PUR-SR holds an approval limit of £500,000 gross including VAT per document under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] [^r2][^r9].
* Superseded rule: Under superseded v2.0 of [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]], role PUR-SR (Senior buyer) held an approval limit of £400,000 gross including VAT [^r7].
* Sources and dates: POL-APR-002 v3.0 (2026-04-01) and POL-APR-002 v3.1 (2026-10-26) vs. Approval_Matrix_v2.0_2024.docx (2024).
* Kind: superseded.

Under superseded version v2.2 of [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]], senior buyers could propose new suppliers, check UN 38.3 test reports prior to first orders, and perform quarterly supplier reviews covering on-time delivery, rejection rates, and pricing [^r8].
