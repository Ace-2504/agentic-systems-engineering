The PUR-EXEC (Purchase executive) role handles purchase approvals and vendor onboarding operations within Northbridge Cycles. As of 26 October 2026, holders of this role have an approval limit of £300,000 gross including VAT per document.[^r6] They are also responsible for initiating supplier onboarding dossiers subject to strict anti-bribery standards.[^r1][^r3]

## Role and limits

The PUR-EXEC role has an approval limit of £300,000 gross including VAT per document as of 2026-10-26 under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002 Approval Matrix for Purchasing]] v3.1.[^r6] A purchase executive (role PUR-EXEC) or senior buyer initiates new vendor onboarding by preparing written quotations, justification, and duplicate search evidence.[^r3] Purchasing staff assigned to this role are strictly prohibited from accepting any gift, regardless of value, from any supplier participating in an active quotation or tender.[^r1]
