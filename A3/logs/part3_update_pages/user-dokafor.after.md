Daniel Okafor is the Purchase Manager at Northbridge Cycles and holds an approval limit of £1,000,000 gross [^r1]. He manages [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]] and holds sole purchasing authority for onboarding approvals, supplier unblocking requests, and quotation dispensations [^r4][^r13][^r18]. As a primary document owner across procurement, he maintains and approves core purchasing policies and standard operating procedures [^r3][^r5][^r7][^r8][^r9][^r12][^r18].

## Role and limits

Daniel Okafor acts in the Ledgerline role of Purchase Manager ([[role-pur-mgr|Role PUR-MGR]]) with a standard financial approval limit of up to £1,000,000 gross [^r1]. In this role, he serves as the direct manager to [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]], approving purchase orders exceeding £250,000 gross up to his £1,000,000 limit [^r2][^r18].

He holds sole authority to approve supplier onboarding files and to submit written requests to IT for unblocking suppliers [^r4][^r15]. Under onboarding procedures, he authorizes requests by signing box 7 of form NBC-F-112 before a master record can be created [^r6]. Additionally, he must issue written dispensation before form NBC-F-118 can be signed whenever fewer than three quotations are obtained for requirements exceeding £100,000 gross [^r13]. Purchasing staff must also notify him whenever a battery supplier fails to submit a renewed UN 38.3 test report 60 days prior to certificate expiry [^r11].
