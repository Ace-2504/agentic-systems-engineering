Henrik Weber is the [[role-plant-head|Plant Head]] at Northbridge Cycles as of 26 October 2026 [^r1][^r24]. Under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]], he approves purchasing orders exceeding £1,000,000 up to £5,000,000 gross [^r24]. In his executive capacity, he serves as the final approver for plant-wide operational policies and standard operating procedures across manufacturing, purchasing, quality, IT, and finance [^r1][^r6][^r7][^r10][^r12].

## Role and limits

Henrik Weber exercises purchase approval authority for orders exceeding £1,000,000 up to £5,000,000 gross [^r24]. Advance approval from the Plant Head is required for any hospitality valued at more than £150 per head [^r2].
