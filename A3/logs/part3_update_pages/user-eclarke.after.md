Emma Clarke is a purchase executive at Northbridge Cycles who operates under user ID ECLARKE [^r1][^r2]. Under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] v3.1, her purchase orders up to £300,000 gross are self-approved upon submission [^r2]. Orders exceeding her limit, such as an order of £285,600 gross under earlier rules, route to her manager [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] [^r1].

## Role and limits

Emma Clarke holds the role of purchase executive ([[role-pur-exec|PUR-EXEC]]) [^r1][^r2]. As of 2026-10-26, orders submitted by Emma Clarke up to £300,000 gross are self-approved [^r2]. Under prior matrix rules, her approval limit was £250,000 gross, causing an order of £285,600 gross to route to [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] [^r1].
