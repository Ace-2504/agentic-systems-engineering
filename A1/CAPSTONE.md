# CAPSTONE: Ask Northbridge for accounts payable

Built on the course's **Finance · payables** module kit (`fin_payables`), with my own changes to its sandbox. All companies, people and amounts are fictional; amounts are in rupees.

## Summary

1. **The process and the people.** Paying a supplier's invoice at Northbridge Cycles, on the course's Finance · payables kit, because it has the same failure pattern as my own Before: money leaves after checks, and "paid" isn't really paid. My own hostel refund is hard to sandbox on its own: it spans EduDesk, four office modules, the Accounts ERP and the bank, and faking all of them is far beyond the course's budget for one module (3 screens, about 8 services), so I use the course's kit option. Priya Shah (accounts payable), Emma Clarke (purchasing) and Meera Kulkarni (tax manager) speak to the assistant; Daniel Okafor, Ganesh Pawar and Vikram Nair decide in their own logins; Treasury gives the rate the money would earn. The record lives in Ledgerline, the ERP.
2. **The sandbox.** The kit's Ledgerline (44 records, hold codes with release lists, Friday payment runs, and its traps: the silent park, two invoices numbered 4471, the resent 4471-R), with my changes: Indian setting (Rs., GST 18%, TDS), a contract and Voltway's discount offer, 260 packs damaged in transit and 40 defective, an insurer to notify, a draft queue where a person approves every entry within 24 hours, a bank statement, return file and bank-alerts mailbox with a spoofed email, a treasury mailbox, and the tax data (e-invoice numbers, GSTR-2B, a reverse-charge table, TDS challans).
3. **Five activities.** `invoice_status`: find an invoice and say whose it is. `explain_hold`: why it is on hold, who can clear it, by when. `enter_invoice`: draft an invoice or credit note for approval. `refer_hold`: send a hold to the person allowed to clear it. `schedule_payment`: draft a payment for a Friday run and record the bank's answer.
4. **Two never-events.** Nothing is written, released or approved without the right person's recorded authority. The assistant never says paid, scheduled, released or posted while anything is held, parked, waiting, still a draft or not confirmed by the bank, and never counts GST credit that GSTR-2B has not confirmed.
5. **Open questions.** How do we stop Priya rubber-stamping drafts she approves 98% of the time? And when the bank's email and statement disagree, how long does the agent wait, and which wins?

**The worked example:** today the insurer hears about the 260 damaged packs 10 days late, Voltway's 0.5% discount on invoice 4471 lapses while the invoice sits on hold, and the team chases the 0.25% instead, which at Treasury's 6.5% a year costs Rs.10,678 more than it saves. With the assistant, the insurance claim goes out on the day of delivery, the holds clear in a day, and the agent asks Treasury: the 0.5% is worth only 5.55% a year on the money paid early, so taking it would cost Rs.20,392 more than it saves, and Vikram declines. 4471 is paid in the 13th November run, the last before the due date; its GST credit is claimed only after GSTR-2B confirms it (Rs.42,46,300.80; the damaged packs' Rs.62,375.04 reversed, the defective packs' GST cancelled by Voltway's credit note); the freight's Rs.6,000 reverse-charge GST and the TDS are paid on time; and "paid" is said only after the bank confirms.

The full design behind this summary (the people, the money, the approval loop, the bank check, the tax steps, guardrails, tests and what changes without new code) is in `CAPSTONE_design.md`, and the picture is `CAPSTONE_SYSTEM_DESIGN_v13.excalidraw`.
