# The source text: Kestrel's hostel deposit refund policy

This is the "one or two pages of real text from your domain" that Part 1 asks for. Kestrel Institute of Technology is the fictional college from A1, and this policy is written to match the A1 Before (`../A1/BEFORE.md`) exactly. Its wording and structure follow the way real Indian colleges publish these rules: refund FAQs, no-dues forms and caution-money circulars. No real student or staff data is used.

It is also the **written rule** that Parts 2 and 3 refer to. Note that it promises things the systems do not do. For example, it says the refund goes to the account on the refund form, but in A1 the ERP paid the old account on the student record. That gap is deliberate: it is how real policies drift from the systems they describe.

KESTREL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
छात्रावास सुरक्षा जमा राशि वापसी नीति
Hostel Security Deposit: No-Dues and Refund Policy

Document KIT/ACC/POL-07 · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 July 2024 · Owner: Accounts Office (Student Deposits)
Supersedes KIT/ACC/POL-07 v3.1 (Aug 2022). Approved by the Finance Committee, meeting FC-24/03.

1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to every student allotted a room in a Kestrel hostel (H1 to H6) who has paid the hostel security deposit of Rs.15,000 at admission.
1.2 It does not cover the tuition caution money (see KIT/ACC/POL-04) or the mess advance, which is adjusted against the final mess bill.

2. When a refund may be requested

2.1 A student may apply on EduDesk after their last end-semester examination, or on the day the Dean of Students approves an early vacation (Form HSL-VAC-02).
2.2 Requests must be made within 6 months of vacating the room. Deposits not claimed within 12 months of vacating are credited to the Student Welfare Fund and are not refundable.
2.3 Students on a semester exchange keep their deposit and do not apply.

3. How to apply

3.1 Log in to EduDesk and open Student Services → No-Dues & Deposit Refund.
3.2 Enter your enrollment number (for example 22CSE0417), hostel and room code (for example H3-214), and the savings bank account number and IFSC in your own name. Joint accounts, NRE/NRO accounts and wallets are not accepted.
3.3 EduDesk verifies the account with a test credit of Rs.1. If the test fails, correct the details and submit again.
3.4 On submission you receive a request number in the format REQ-YY-NNNN (for example REQ-25-0917). Quote it in every message.

4. Clearances

4.1 The request is sent automatically to four sections. Each must mark its line "Clear" before the request moves to Accounts:
  (a) Library: books returned; overdue fines (Rs.10 per day per book) are recorded as a deduction.
  (b) Labs: no equipment issued and no breakage charges outstanding (lab inventory module).
  (c) Hostel: room inspection by the caretaker, approved by the Warden; damage charged as per the Damage Rate Card (Annexure A); room key returned.
  (d) Mess: mess card returned at the mess office and the final mess bill settled.
4.2 Inspections are done by appointment with the caretaker, at least 48 hours before you vacate.
4.3 You may accept or dispute a proposed damage charge on EduDesk within 3 working days. Disputed charges are decided by the Warden, whose decision is final.

5. Payment of the refund

5.1 When all four lines are Clear, EduDesk shows "Forwarded to Accounts". The Accounts Office raises a refund voucher (RV-YY-NNNN) for the deposit less all recorded deductions.
5.2 Refunds are credited by NEFT to the bank account given on the refund form, normally within 30 working days of the request being forwarded to Accounts.
5.3 When EduDesk shows "Refund paid" with a UTR number, the refund has been transferred to your bank account. Please allow up to 2 working days for it to reflect.
5.4 If a refund is returned by the bank because of incorrect or closed account details, it will be paid again after the student updates the details. The Institute is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect bank details.
5.5 No refund is paid in cash or by cheque.

6. Queries and grievances

6.1 Raise a ticket on the EduDesk helpdesk under Category: Accounts → Deposit Refund, quoting your REQ number. The target response time is 2 working days.
6.2 If the ticket is not resolved within 10 working days, write to the Accounts Officer at refunds@kit-kestrel.edu.in. Appeals go to the Head of Finance.
